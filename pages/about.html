🎓 Academic Project & Team Overview

EcoLoop is designed and developed as a working prototype for the Formative Assessment (FA-2) in E-Waste Management & Circular Sustainability.

Formative Assessment 2 (FA-2)

Course: Solid & Hazardous Waste Management

Academic Year 2026 • Engineering Demonstration

This web application was engineered to translate theoretical principles of electronic waste segregation, toxic component isolation, urban mining economics, and extended producer responsibility (EPR) into an interactive software prototype.

⚠️

1. Problem Statement

The exponential pace of consumer electronics upgrades has made e-waste the fastest-growing municipal waste stream globally (generating over 62 million metric tonnes per year).

  • • Absence of formal collection channels: Over 78% of household e-waste ends up in landfills or informal backyard acid baths.
  • • Severe environmental pollution: Heavy metals (Lead, Cadmium, Mercury) leach into drinking groundwater aquifers.
  • • Massive resource waste: Billions of dollars worth of rare earth elements, gold, and pure copper are lost forever.
💡

2. The EcoLoop Solution

EcoLoop provides an accessible digital bridge between consumers and certified e-waste recyclers to institutionalize circular resource recovery.

  • • Digital Registration: Streamlined collection scheduling with instant weight estimation and unique request tracking IDs.
  • • Smart Segregation: Rule-based wizard classifying items into reusable, repairable, recyclable, and hazardous streams.
  • • Economic Transparency: Built-in metallurgical calculator showing real recovery yields and secondary market valuations.

🎯 Core Academic Objectives

1. Operational Workflow

Demonstrate a complete lifecycle loop from citizen registration to authorized smelting and closed-loop manufacturing.

2. Source Segregation

Educate users on isolating hazardous batteries and cold-cathode lamps from regular waste streams before collection.

3. Circular Economics

Show mathematically how secondary urban mining of PCBs yields higher precious metal concentrations than natural mining ores.

4. Verified Transparency

Provide transparent calculation disclosures distinguishing measured weights from model-based statistical assumptions.
