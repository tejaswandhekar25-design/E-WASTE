⚙️ Technical Operations

Inside a Certified E-Waste Recycling Facility

How hazardous contaminants are safely contained while valuable minerals are recovered through industrial processing.

1. Manual Depollution & Decontamination

Trained technicians remove batteries, mercury backlights, toner cartridges, and large capacitors to isolate toxic streams before shredding.

2. Multi-Stage Mechanical Shredding

Slow-speed rotary shear shredders fragment remaining materials into uniform 2-inch fractions under HEPA-filtered vacuum hoods.

3. Magnetic & Eddy Current Sorting

High-powered cross-belt neodymium magnets lift iron and steel. Rotating eddy current rotors launch non-ferrous aluminum and copper forward.

4. Optical & Density Separation

Near-infrared (NIR) sensors sort engineering polymers (ABS, Polycarbonate, Polystyrene), while air classifiers separate light plastics from metallic dust.

5. Hydrometallurgical Leaching

Eco-friendly chemical leaching dissolves microscopic gold and silver layers from circuit board pins without atmospheric air emissions.

6. Smelting & Electrolytic Refining

Copper concentrates are melted in high-temperature furnaces and refined into 99.99% pure electrolytic cathode sheets for electrical cables.
